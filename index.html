Abdelrahman Amer

I am a Postdoctoral Researcher in the Department of Economics at the University of Konstanz. I received my PhD in Economics from the University of Toronto in 2026.

My research lies at the intersection of Labor and Spatial Economics. I study the role of worker preferences and frictions in shaping labor market power and earnings inequality across individuals and locations.
